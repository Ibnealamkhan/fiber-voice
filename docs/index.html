Features

Everything a desk phone does, built into your Mac.

HD calls from your number

Outgoing and incoming calls in HD voice, a keypad for phone menus, mute, and a call timer. Type or paste a number anywhere and press Return.

Recents & missed calls

Every call is grouped by day, with a red badge on the sidebar, Dock and menu bar for calls you missed. Call anyone back with one click.

Contacts & favourites

See names from your Mac’s Contacts on calls and in the log. Star favourites, add contacts of your own, and see your call history with each person.

Do Not Disturb

Silence your Mac for an hour, until morning, or every night on a schedule. If the same number calls twice in 3 minutes, the second call can ring through.

Block numbers

Reject unwanted callers straight from Recents or Contacts. Blocked calls are kept in the log, so you can see who tried.

Private by design

Everything runs on your Mac and talks only to your own router. Nothing is recorded or uploaded, and no account is needed.

Menu bar

Line status, recent calls and Do Not Disturb, always one click away.

Answer from banners

Incoming-call notifications with Answer and Decline buttons.

Click to call

Click any phone number in Safari, Mail or Contacts to call it.

Opens at login

Starts quietly in the menu bar, so your landline can always ring.

Before you start

What you need

  • JioFiber landlineFixed-line calling must be active on your JioFiber connection.
  • Your home networkConnect your Mac to your JioFiber Wi-Fi or LAN, without a VPN or custom DNS.
  • IPv6 turned onIt’s on by default. If setup says otherwise, turn it on in the router’s settings.
  • Registered mobileThe router texts a one-time code to it during setup.

Install

Install in four steps

It takes about two minutes. You only do this once.

  1. Download the app

    Click Download for Mac. You get a file called Fiber-Voice-mac-arm64.dmg (about 40 MB) in your Downloads folder.

    Not sure your Mac has Apple silicon? Open the Apple menu ▸ About This Mac. If Chip says Apple M1, M2, M3 or newer, you’re set. Intel Macs can build their own copy.

  2. Drag it into Applications

    Double-click the downloaded file. In the window that opens, drag Fiber Voice onto the Applications folder. Then eject the “Fiber Voice” disk in Finder’s sidebar.

    →

  3. Open it the first time

    Open Fiber Voice from Applications or Launchpad. This free app isn’t notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch:

    • Click Done (or OK) on the warning.
    • Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and scroll down.
    • Next to “Fiber Voice was blocked…”, click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.

    You only need to do this once. The full source code is on GitHub if you’d rather check it or build it yourself.

  4. Allow what it asks for

    • Local network: click Allow. The app needs this to reach your router.
    • Notifications: allow them, so incoming and missed calls can alert you.
    • Microphone: it asks on your first call. Click Allow.
    • Open at login: say yes, so your landline rings whenever your Mac is on.

Setup

Connect your landline (about a minute)

The first time you open Fiber Voice, it shows a short setup screen. Have your registered mobile nearby.

  1. Find your router

    Click Check router. Fiber Voice finds your JioFiber router and shows your fixed-line number and the router’s security certificate.

    Click Trust this router. Only do this on your own home network. Your Mac will only ever send your calling credentials to this exact router.

Everyday use

Good to know

Calling

Type or paste a number anywhere in the window, then press Return. During a call: Mute, Keypad for “press 1” menus, and Esc to hang up. Headphones give the best sound.

Incoming calls

The window pops up, a ringtone plays, and a notification offers Answer and Decline. For buttons that stay on screen, set Fiber Voice to Alerts in System Settings ▸ Notifications.

Closing isn’t quitting

Close the window and Fiber Voice keeps running in the menu bar, so calls still ring. Quit with ⌘Q to stop receiving calls on this Mac.

Click-to-call links

In the menu bar icon, choose Use for Phone Links. Phone numbers you click in Safari, Mail, Contacts or Calendar will then open in Fiber Voice.

Help

Troubleshooting

“Cannot find the JioFiber router”

Connect your Mac directly to your JioFiber Wi-Fi or a LAN cable from the JioFiber router. Turn off any VPN, and remove custom DNS servers such as 8.8.8.8 or 1.1.1.1 in System Settings ▸ Network ▸ Details ▸ DNS. A mesh system or second router with its own network can hide the JioFiber router.

Setup says IPv6 is “Not available”

Open your router’s admin page (usually http://192.168.29.1), turn on IPv6, then turn your Mac’s Wi-Fi off and on again and click Check again.

The code never arrives, or setup is refused
  • Wait a minute, then click Send a new code. It goes to the mobile number registered with your JioFiber account.
  • If the router refuses, it may have reached its device limit. Remove an old device from the JioJoin app’s device list and try again.
  • “Encrypted configuration” means your router firmware isn’t supported yet.
Calls fail, or show “Unreachable”

Hover over the call in Recents to see the exact reason the network gave. “Unreachable” means Jio reported the other phone as switched off or out of coverage. If every call fails, check that the sidebar says Ready for calls, use Line ▸ Reload, and make sure landline calling works from your phone.

No sound, or the other person can’t hear me

Allow microphone access in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Microphone ▸ Fiber Voice. Try the microphone test on the Connection page, and use headphones to avoid echo.

I changed routers or want to remove my details

Click Log out in the sidebar. This disconnects the Mac, forgets the router, and clears the call log and settings saved in the app; your Mac’s Contacts are never changed. To uninstall, delete Fiber Voice from Applications, along with ~/Library/Application Support/Fiber Voice.

Is this safe? Does it record my calls?

Fiber Voice runs entirely on your Mac and talks only to your own JioFiber router. It records no calls and stores no audio. The call log keeps only numbers, times and durations, on your Mac. Your credentials are stored with owner-only permissions. The source code is open for anyone to read.
